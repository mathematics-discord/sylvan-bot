# v1.0.4 - 2026-11-10
## Moderation
### Changed
- For <@&1522665207225127034> and above, `/ticket show` now names its ticket lookup option `ticket_id` and clarifies that it accepts a ticket ID, not a user ID.
- Administrators control new ticket capture and Automod with the same enabled switch. Disabling it pauses normal ticket management and delivery while existing bans, tracked roles, and long timeouts continue to be checked and lifted or renewed. `/ticket repair` can inspect an uncertain publication, adopt an existing Sylvan message, or explicitly retry sending, including while the system is disabled.
- <@&1328097118644338779> and above can edit and approve anyone's tickets; their own actions are accepted automatically through View Audit Log, with no exemption role list. Sylvan's Automod bans and timeouts require approval, including extensions of accepted timeouts. Image-detector bans remain automatically accepted, and notes never require approval. Duration changes by other moderators return tickets to review; comment edits and automatic renewals preserve approval. The review summary stays at the bottom of the tickets channel.
- <@&1522665207225127034> and above receive an Edit DM for every captured human action, even when its details are complete. Failed initial DMs retry without holding up other tickets. Timeout extensions and reductions keep the same active ticket, including timeouts longer than 28 days. Moderators can edit their own notes; editing another moderator's note requires View Audit Log.
- <@&1522665074571870419> and above must lift server voice mutes and deafens manually in Discord. Their tickets remain available for review, but durations are descriptive and no longer trigger automatic removal. Wait for the recorded reversal before hiding a voice ticket.
### Fixed
- <@&1522665207225127034> and above can change the duration of an active timeout even when its imported record is paused or differs from Discord. A successful edit resumes automatic enforcement. Paused tickets now explain how to resume them.
## Internal
### Changed
- Replace ticket strategy dispatch with direct capture and enforcement operations, separate enforcement from publication and DM delivery, and use typed pending operation kinds. Retire completed import flags, ordered publication, and legacy format parsing. Forward migrations preserve ticket records, raw history, existing publication references, prior review acceptance, and pending initial prompts.
- Keep chained-call dots aligned with C# continuation indentation and put object initializer members on separate lines. Apply the readability cleanup to ticket tests.
- Split ticket audit parsing and recording into focused methods on the reader and coordinator, replace compound control-flow conditions with named predicates, initialise observations through required named properties, and use Discord.NET's snowflake timestamp helper.
- Use namespace-scoped DotNext imports to write Optional<T> without qualification in Discord-facing code, retaining the existing optional type and behaviour.
