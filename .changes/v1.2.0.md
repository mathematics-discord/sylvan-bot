# v1.2.0 - 2026-11-14
## General
### Added
- Members can use Subscribe and Unsubscribe at the bottom of the announcements channel to choose whether they receive announcement notifications.
## Administration
### Added
- Administrators can use `/config announcements channel:` to select the announcements channel. Sylvan creates and manages the 📯 subscription role and keeps the subscription buttons below new messages.
## Moderation
### Added
- <@&1328097118644338779> and above can use `/announce` to send a titled announcement to subscribers. The form supports Discord Markdown and mentions, with a 100-character title and a 3,850-character body; only the 📯 subscription role is notified.
