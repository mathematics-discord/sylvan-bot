## I still have the role outside my hours

- **You turned your schedule off.** The default gives you
  <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> around the clock. If one of your
  staff roles is exempt, turning off the schedule instead leaves the ping
  role as it was.
- **Your schedule is on.** Check the time zone and both ends of the window.
  A fixed offset stays the same when local clocks change. An overnight
  window continues into the next day. To correct it, [submit your hours
  again](../../staff/working-hours/#work-overnight-or-change-your-hours).
- **Everyone’s schedule seems to have stopped.** Ask an administrator to
  check `/config working-hours show`. A server-wide pause restores the
  usual unscheduled defaults.

## Sylvan rejected my times

Include seconds on both times, for example `09:00:00`. With local times,
choose a named `timezone`. With a fixed offset, include it on both times
and leave `timezone` empty. A window shorter than 20 minutes is rejected,
including one where the start and end are identical. A rejected submission
leaves your previous schedule in effect.

For accepted formats and examples, see the [personal command](../../commands/working-hours/).

## The role update is pending

If the reply starts with **Saved.**, Sylvan has recorded the change and will
retry the role update. Give it time, then check the roles on your Discord
profile. Manually changing the ping role while your schedule is on only
lasts until Sylvan next applies your hours.

If the update stays pending, an administrator can use
[`/config working-hours show`](../../commands/working-conditions/#config-working-hours-show)
to check that the ping role still exists and sits below Sylvan’s highest role.
The reply also flags a missing **Manage Roles** permission or unavailable member data.

## I can’t set a schedule

Run `/working-hours` in the server. If the command is missing or Sylvan
says you need an eligible staff role, ask an administrator to check that
your moderator role is included in the setup and that you have **Mute
Members**. If scheduling is disabled, an administrator must enable it
before anyone can change their hours.

If you leave the server or lose your last eligible role, Sylvan disables
your personal schedule when it detects the change. Once you’re eligible
again, [set your hours](../../staff/working-hours/) to resume.

If the reply says member data is unavailable and **no preference was changed**,
try the command again once Sylvan has reconnected.
