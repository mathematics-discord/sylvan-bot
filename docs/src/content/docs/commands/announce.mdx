Available to <RoleMention id="1328097118644338779" /> and above.

Run `/announce` to open the announcement form. Enter a **Title** of up to
100 characters and a **Body** of up to 3,850 characters, then submit it.
Sylvan posts the announcement in the configured channel and privately confirms
that it was sent, with a link to the message.

The announcement starts with a heading containing the `📯` role mention,
an em dash, and your title, followed by the body on the next line.
Markdown in both fields is preserved. Leading heading hashes in the title
are removed; a title such as `C# updates` stays unchanged.

Role, user, and channel mentions display normally. Only the `📯` subscription
role receives a notification; other mentions, including `@everyone` and
`@here`, do not notify anyone.

## Subscribe to announcements

Use **Subscribe** at the bottom of the announcements channel to receive the
`📯` role, or **Unsubscribe** to remove it. Sylvan confirms your choice privately.
The buttons move below new posts, including messages sent directly by staff
or other bots.

If administrators have added the role to Channels & Roles, you can also
change your subscription there. Both methods change the same role.

An administrator must [configure announcements](../config-announcements/)
before `/announce` can be used.
