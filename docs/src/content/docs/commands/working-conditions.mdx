<RoleMention id="administrators" /> can run these commands in the server.
All replies are private. For first-time configuration, follow
[Set up Working Hours](../../setup/working-hours/). Moderators set their own
hours with [`/working-hours`](../working-hours/).

## Check the current configuration

### `/config working-hours show`

Takes no options. Shows whether scheduling is enabled, the ping role, the
eligible and exempt roles, and any problems Sylvan can identify.

## Set up the roles

### `/config working-hours ping-role`

Requires `role`. Select the role Sylvan will give and remove. Scheduling
must be disabled to change it. Choose a role below Sylvan’s highest role.
The ping role cannot be `@everyone`, integration-managed, or an eligible
staff role. Previous assignments of the old ping role stay in place.

```text
/config working-hours ping-role role:@Moderators
```

### `/config working-hours eligible-role`

Requires `action` (**add** or **remove**) and `role`. Holding any listed
role makes a moderator eligible to set a schedule. Choose a staff role
other than `@everyone` or the ping role.

```text
/config working-hours eligible-role action:add role:@Trial Moderators
```

To remove a role, choose **remove**. Remove its exemption first if it has
one. If a moderator loses their last eligible role while scheduling is
running, Sylvan disables their schedule and removes their ping role.
Disable scheduling before removing the server’s last eligible role.

## Set exemptions for staff without a schedule

### `/config working-hours exempt-role`

Requires `action` (**add** or **remove**) and `role`. Add an already
eligible role to let its holders manage their ping role manually when they
have no active schedule. An enabled personal schedule still takes priority.

```text
/config working-hours exempt-role action:remove role:@Senior Moderators
```

## Enable or suspend scheduling

### `/config working-hours enable`

Takes no options. Requires a valid ping role, **Manage Roles** for Sylvan,
and at least one eligible role. Saved, enabled schedules resume from the
current time.

### `/config working-hours disable`

Takes no options. Suspends personal schedules across the server and
restores the [unscheduled role defaults](../../features/working-hours/#when-do-i-have-the-role).
Personal schedules are retained. The reply indicates whether the role
updates have completed or will be retried.
