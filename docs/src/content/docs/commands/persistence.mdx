---
title: Restore roles when members rejoin
description: Select ordinary server roles to remember on departure and check whether Sylvan can restore them.
---

Use `/config persistence` in the server with Discord’s **Administrator**
permission. Adding and removing roles gives you a private confirmation.
**Listing roles posts the result in the channel.**

## Remember a role

1. Choose an ordinary role, such as Art. Sylvan checks server permissions
   and channel and category overrides for staff or moderation powers.
   Discord-managed roles and `@everyone` cannot be selected.
2. Give Sylvan **Manage Roles** and put its highest role above Art. Unless
   you own the server, Art must also sit below your highest role.
3. Run the command with the role you want Sylvan to remember.

```text
/config persistence add role:@Art
```

From now on, when Sylvan sees a member leave with Art, it can
[restore Art when they rejoin](../../features/persistence/). It needs the
member’s roles at departure. Enabling restoration after someone has left
can’t recover their old roles.

## Check which roles can be restored

Run `/config persistence list` in a channel where you want the list to
appear. A **restoration blocked** entry means the role has unsafe
permissions or Sylvan can no longer manage it. A deleted role appears by
its Discord ID.

If a member is missing a role, check the role’s permissions and hierarchy
first. A failed Discord update or a missing bot permission can leave a
restoration pending. Sylvan discards saved roles that were deleted or
became unsafe. If the role was a tracked restriction, Sylvan also checks
its ticket before restoring it. Ended or expired restrictions stay off.

## Stop remembering a role

```text
/config persistence remove role:@Art
```

Removing Art clears its pending restorations. Members who already have
Art keep it, and Sylvan stops remembering it on future departures.
