Available to <RoleMention id="administrators" />.

Run `/config announcements channel:#announcements`, selecting a text or
announcement channel in this server. Sylvan creates a role named `📯` and
places **Subscribe** and **Unsubscribe** buttons at the bottom of the channel.

The bot needs **View Channel**, **Send Messages**, and **Read Message History**
in the channel, plus **Mention @everyone, @here, and All Roles** to notify
subscribers. It also needs **Manage Roles**, with its highest role above `📯`.
The subscription role has no server permissions and is not publicly mentionable.

## Change or repair the setup

Run the command again to choose another channel. Existing subscribers keep
their role, and the subscription buttons move to the new channel. Previously
published announcements remain where they were sent.

Running the command again also recreates the subscription role if it was
deleted. In that case, members must subscribe again, and an administrator
must update the role choice in Onboarding.

See [/announce](../announce/) for writing announcements and subscribing.
