Every moderator on Mathematics can use `/working-hours` in the server to
choose when they hold the pingable <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" />
role. The command changes your own schedule and replies privately.
For a walkthrough, see [Set your working hours](../../staff/working-hours/).

## Set your hours

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| `enable` | Required. Choose **True** to set or replace your daily hours. Choose **False** on its own to turn them off. |
| `start` | Required when enabling. The time you start holding the role, in 24-hour `HH:MM:SS` format. |
| `end` | Required when enabling. The time you stop holding the role, in the same format. |
| `timezone` | For local times, select a named zone such as `Europe/London` from autocomplete. Omit this field when both times include a UTC offset. |

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:09:00:00 end:17:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

Each submission with **True** replaces the whole schedule. Include both
times even if you’re changing only one.

## Use a fixed UTC offset

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:09:00:00+01:00 end:17:00:00+01:00
```

This runs from 08:00 to 16:00 UTC all year. Give both times the same numeric
offset and omit `timezone`. For UTC itself, use `Z` on both times, such as
`09:00:00Z` and `17:00:00Z`.

## Time format and overnight hours

Use two digits each for hours (00–23), minutes (00–59), and seconds (00–59).
You can add fractional seconds, as in `09:00:00.5`. Enter just the time,
without a date or spaces. For zero offset, use `Z` or `+00:00`.

A window must last at least 20 minutes. An end earlier than the start falls
on the following day. Equal start and end times count as zero minutes.

## While your schedule is enabled

A reply beginning **Saved.** confirms the schedule was recorded. The next
line tells you whether Sylvan has finished applying the role. Any HR or
night-shift commentary that follows is part of the confirmation.

## Disable your schedule

```text
/working-hours enable:False
```

Omit `start`, `end`, and `timezone`. Your role returns to the
[unscheduled default](../../features/working-hours/#when-do-i-have-the-role).
Most moderators hold it all day. Exempt moderators keep manual control.
