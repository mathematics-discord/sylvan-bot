Use `/working-hours` to set a daily availability window on <a href="https://discord.gg/math" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mathematics</a>.
Sylvan adds <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> during those hours and removes it
outside them. Your moderation powers and colours are unaffected.

Working Hours is available to <RoleMention id="1328097118644338779" />,
<RoleMention id="1522665074571870419"/>, and <RoleMention id="1522665207225127034"/>.
Run the command inside Mathematics. Replies are visible only to you.

## Set your hours

Supply `enable:true`, a start time, an end time, and your timezone:

```text
/working-hours enable:true start:09:00:00 end:17:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

This makes you available from 9am to 5pm every day, including weekends.
Select your timezone from the autocomplete suggestions. Named timezones
follow daylight-saving changes automatically, so your hours stay tied
to the local clock.

The options are:

- `enable`: required. Use `true` to set your hours or `false` to disable
your schedule.
- `start`: when your daily availability begins. Required when enabling.
- `end`: when your daily availability ends. Required when enabling.
- `timezone`: a named timezone such as `Europe/London`. Required unless
both times include a fixed UTC offset.

To change your hours, run the command again with the complete replacement
schedule. You cannot change just the start time, end time, or timezone.

## Use a fixed UTC offset

Alternatively, put the same UTC offset on both times and leave out
`timezone`:

```text
/working-hours enable:true start:09:00:00+01:00 end:17:00:00+01:00
```

Fixed offsets stay fixed throughout the year. This example always uses
UTC+1, even when your local clocks change. You can also use `Z` for UTC:

```text
/working-hours enable:true start:09:00:00Z end:17:00:00Z
```

Do not combine a named timezone with fixed offsets. Both times must
either omit offsets or include numerically equal offsets.

## Time format and overnight hours

Use 24-hour `HH:MM:SS` times. Seconds are required: write `09:00:00`,
not `09:00`. Fractional seconds, such as `09:00:00.5`, are also accepted.
Do not include dates or spaces. Hours must be 00–23, and minutes and
seconds must be 00–59. Leap seconds and the offset `-00:00` are not accepted.

Your window must last at least 20 minutes. If the end time is earlier
than the start time, the window continues into the next day:

```text
/working-hours enable:true start:22:00:00 end:07:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

This makes you available from 10pm until 7am the following morning,
repeating every day. Equal start and end times are not accepted as an
all-day schedule.

## While your schedule is enabled

Sylvan controls your <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> role according to your hours and overrides
manual changes to that role. This also applies to exempt senior staff
who choose to enable a schedule.

The start time is included in your availability window; the end time is
not. Role changes may take a short time to appear. If the reply says your
schedule was saved but role updates are pending, the schedule is saved
and Sylvan still needs to finish applying the role change.

## Disable your schedule

```text
/working-hours enable:false
```

Do not include `start`, `end`, or `timezone`. This restores permanent
availability for ordinary staff. Staff with a configured exemption role
retain manual control of <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> instead.

To enable a schedule again, supply the complete schedule with `enable:true`.
