Sylvan handles moderation records, private mail and member roles on the [Mathematics Discord server](https://discord.gg/math).

## For members

- [Contact the moderators privately](./features/sylvanmail/) to report a problem, ask about a moderation decision or send a screenshot.
- [Choose your display colour](./features/colours/) or switch back to your usual colour.
- [Get a role by reacting to a message](./features/reaction-roles/). If you’ve just rejoined, check [which roles come back automatically](./features/persistence/).

## Staff handbook

| What are you working on? | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| A moderation ticket | [Complete a ticket](./staff/tickets/complete-a-ticket/), [find a member’s history](./staff/tickets/find-a-ticket/) or [review the queue](./staff/tickets/review-tickets/). The [ticket handbook](./staff/tickets/) covers the full workflow. |
| A message from a member | [Reply from the private ModMail forum](./staff/sylvanmail/) and check that your reply was delivered. |
| A moderation incident | [Read edit and deletion logs](./features/logging/), [investigate an Automod match](./features/automod/) or [check an attachment-spam ban](./features/image-spam/). |
| Your availability | [Set your working hours](./staff/working-hours/) to choose when a moderator ping includes you. |

## Server setup

Start with the feature you’re configuring.

- **Staff channels and roles** — [Ticket capture](./setup/tickets/), [ModMail](./setup/sylvanmail/), [message logs](./commands/logging/) and the [moderator ping role](./setup/working-hours/).
- **Member roles** — [Display colours](./commands/color-admin/), [reaction roles](./commands/role-react/) and [roles restored on rejoin](./commands/persistence/).
- **Automatic moderation** — [Automod actions and exemptions](./commands/automod-admin/) and [attachment-spam thresholds](./commands/spam/).
