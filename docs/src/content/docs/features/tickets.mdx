A ticket is a numbered record of a moderation action or staff note. It
identifies the member, the responsible moderator, and what happened. Use the
[ticket handbook](../../staff/tickets/) for steps, or the
[command reference](../../commands/tickets/) for exact fields and shortcuts.

## What gets a ticket?

When tickets are enabled, Sylvan reads the server audit log and records
bans, kicks, timeouts, server voice mutes and deafens, and grants of roles
an administrator has selected for tracking. Take the action in Discord.
Sylvan saves the record and attempts to publish it in the tickets channel.
Staff can also [add a note](../../staff/tickets/add-a-note/) about an
incident that involved no restriction.

An audit reason such as `2 days, repeated invite spam after a warning` can
supply a duration and comment. A timeout starts with the expiry chosen in
Discord. Extensions and reductions update the current ticket. A new timeout
after the previous restriction ends creates a new ticket.

Actions taken by bots can also have tickets. For example, once Sylvan
matches an [Automod ban](../automod/#timed-bans-and-failures) to its audit
entry, use its ticket to change the restriction. Bot actions do not send
a moderator a reminder. Every captured human action gets an initial DM to
complete or adjust its details, even when already complete or accepted.

## Read a ticket

- **Action and subject** identify what happened and to whom. **Moderator ID**
  identifies the responsible moderator, including after someone else edits
  the record.
- **Duration** runs from the original action. **Expires** shows the deadline
  in your local time and how long remains. **Superseded** means a later
  action took over. Use the newer ticket to check the current restriction.
- **Unapproved** and **Approved** describe the review decision. A restriction can be active before approval or expire while its
  ticket still awaits review.
- **Hidden** describes record visibility. Restoring a hidden ticket makes
  it visible again. The restriction stays lifted.
- **Modification history** shows recent moderator changes. **Pending**,
  **Expiry paused** and **Enforcement paused** describe work Sylvan still
  needs to confirm. [Check what each status means](../../help/tickets/).

## Who can do what?

Sylvan uses your current Discord permissions. On Mathematics,
<RoleMention id="1522665207225127034" /> have **Timeout Members**,
<RoleMention id="1522665074571870419" /> have **Ban Members**, and
<RoleMention id="1328097118644338779" /> have **View Audit Log**.
<RoleMention id="administrators" /> can use every ticket command.

| Task | Discord permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Look up visible tickets by slash command | Any of Timeout Members, Ban Members, Kick Members, Mute Members, Deafen Members, Manage Roles, or View Audit Log. |
| Use **Apps → List Tickets** or **Apps → Add Moderation Note** on a member | Timeout Members. |
| Create, edit, or hide a visible note | Timeout Members or View Audit Log. Without View Audit Log, you can only correct or hide your own notes. |
| Correct your own action ticket | The permission for the original action, or View Audit Log. For example, Ban Members for a ban or Manage Roles for a tracked role. |
| Correct someone else’s action, review tickets, read hidden records, hide an action ticket, or restore a record | View Audit Log. |
| Revert an active ban, timeout, or tracked role | Permission to correct its ticket and the matching action permission. [Role hierarchy and Discord’s current state also matter](../../staff/tickets/change-a-restriction/#lift-a-restriction-now). |
| Configure tickets or repair a publication | Administrator. |
