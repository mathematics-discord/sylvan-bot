Every moderator on Mathematics can choose when they hold the pingable
<RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> role. Working Hours adds it during
the hours you choose, so a mention of the role includes you then.
Your staff role and moderation permissions stay with you throughout the day.

[Set your working hours](../../staff/working-hours/) to choose your daily times,
change them, or turn your schedule off.

## When do I have the role?

| Your schedule | What happens to the ping role |
| --- | --- |
| On | Sylvan gives you the role at the start of your hours and removes it at the end. It corrects manual changes, including for moderators with an exemption. |
| Off, or never set | You hold the role around the clock by default. |
| Off, with an exempt staff role | Sylvan leaves your ping role as it is. Staff can add or remove it manually. |

One time window repeats every day, including weekends. If you choose
22:00 to 07:00, you’re included from 22:00 through the night. At 07:00
your window ends. It opens again at 22:00 that evening. Role updates can
take a short time to appear in Discord.

## When the clocks change

Choose a named time zone such as `Europe/London` to keep the same local
hours through daylight-saving changes. If you want the same UTC hours all
year, use a [fixed offset](../../commands/working-hours/#use-a-fixed-utc-offset).

If a clock change skips your chosen start or end time, Sylvan moves that
time forward by the size of the gap. If it occurs twice, Sylvan uses the
first occurrence. For example, a London schedule of 01:15 to 01:45 runs
during the first 01:15 to 01:45 when the clocks go back.

## Defaults and suspension

If an administrator pauses Working Hours for the server, the defaults for
moderators without a schedule apply. Sylvan keeps enabled schedules and
resumes them from the current time when the feature is switched back on.

If the role looks wrong, [check your ping role](../../help/working-hours/).
Administrators can [set up the roles or pause scheduling](../../setup/working-hours/).
