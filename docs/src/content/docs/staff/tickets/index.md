If you’ve just taken a moderation action in Discord, open Sylvan’s DM and
choose **Edit**. If you already have the ticket message open, choose
**Apps → Manage Ticket** to see its details and controls privately.

## Record an incident

- [Complete or correct a ticket](./complete-a-ticket/) after a ban, kick,
  timeout, voice restriction, or tracked role grant.
- [Add a moderation note](./add-a-note/) to record a conversation or warning
  when there’s no action ticket to complete.
- [Find a ticket or member history](./find-a-ticket/) using a member, ticket
  number or message link.

## Review or change a decision

- [Review the ticket queue](./review-tickets/) to approve completed records
  and see which ones still need details.
- [Change a duration or lift a restriction](./change-a-restriction/) to
  correct an expiry or end an action while keeping its record visible.
- [Hide or restore a ticket](./hide-or-restore/) to change whether the
  record appears in ordinary lookups and the tickets channel.

If an update is pending or a ticket post is missing, [check the ticket’s
status](../../help/tickets/) before repeating the action. For exact command
fields, use the [ticket command reference](../../commands/tickets/).
