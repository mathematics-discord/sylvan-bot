Every moderator can choose when they receive <RoleMention id="268886789983436800" />
pings. Run `/working-hours` in the server to set your own schedule. Only you
see the reply.

## Set a daily schedule

1. In Discord, select `/working-hours` and set **enable** to **True**.
2. Enter **start** and **end** in 24-hour time, including seconds. Choose
   your **timezone** from the suggestions. For 9 am to 5 pm in London, enter
   the following command.

   ```text
   /working-hours enable:True start:09:00:00 end:17:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
   ```

3. Look for **Saved.** in the private reply. With these times, Sylvan adds
   the ping role at 09:00 and removes it at 17:00 London time. Check the
   roles on your Discord profile. If the update is pending, Sylvan will retry it.

The same hours apply every day. Choose a window of at least 20 minutes.

## Work overnight or change your hours

To be pingable from 10 pm until 7 am the next morning, enter the earlier
clock time as the end. Replace the time zone with your own.

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:22:00:00 end:07:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

To change your hours again, send the whole schedule with both times and
your time zone or [UTC offset](../../commands/working-hours/#use-a-fixed-utc-offset).
The new schedule takes effect as soon as Sylvan applies it.

## Turn off your schedule

```text
/working-hours enable:False
```

**By default, turning your schedule off makes you pingable around the clock.**
If an administrator has exempted one of your staff roles, Sylvan leaves
your current ping role in place for manual changes. To use a schedule again,
submit your times with **enable** set to **True**.

If your profile still shows the wrong role, see [Troubleshoot Working Hours](../../help/working-hours/).
