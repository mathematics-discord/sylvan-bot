Use `/working-hours` in the server to choose when you hold the
<RoleMention id="268886789983436800" /> ping role on Mathematics. Your
staff role and moderation permissions stay with you throughout the day.
You need **Mute Members** and a staff role that an administrator has made
eligible for scheduling.

<span id="set-your-hours" />

## Set a daily schedule

This example makes you pingable from 9 am to 5 pm UK time every day, including weekends.

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:09:00:00 end:17:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

Choose your time zone from the suggestions and enter both times in
24-hour format, including seconds. You can include a fraction of a second,
such as `09:00:00.5`. Choose a window of at least 20 minutes.

<span id="while-your-schedule-is-enabled" />

Look for **Saved.** in the private reply. Check the roles on your server
profile. In this example, Sylvan adds the ping role at 09:00 London time
and removes it at 17:00. If the reply says the update is pending, Sylvan
has saved the schedule and will retry the role change.

<span id="time-format-and-overnight-hours" />

## Work overnight or change your hours

Submit the whole schedule again to replace it. An end time earlier than
the start means the shift ends the following day. This example covers
10 pm to 7 am.

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:22:00:00 end:07:00:00 timezone:Europe/London
```

<span id="when-the-clocks-change" />

A named time zone keeps the same local hours when the clocks change. If
the clock skips your chosen start or end time, Sylvan shifts that time
forward by the length of the gap. If the clock passes it twice, Sylvan
uses the first occurrence.

### Use a fixed UTC offset

For the same UTC hours year-round, put `Z` on both times and omit
`timezone`. You can also use the same numeric offset on both times. This
example runs from 08:00 to 16:00 UTC throughout the year.

```text
/working-hours enable:True start:09:00:00+01:00 end:17:00:00+01:00
```

## Turn off your schedule

```text
/working-hours enable:False
```

Leave all other fields empty. **Turning off your schedule normally makes
you pingable around the clock.** If one of your eligible staff roles has
an exemption, Sylvan leaves your ping role as it is and staff can change
it manually. The same defaults apply if an administrator pauses schedules
server-wide.

## If your hours aren’t taking effect

### Sylvan rejected my times

Include seconds on both times. Use either a named zone or matching offsets.
Identical times count as a zero-minute window. A rejected submission leaves
your previous hours in effect.

<span id="the-role-update-is-pending" />

### I still have the role outside my hours

Check whether your hours use a named zone or a fixed offset. While your
schedule is on, Sylvan corrects manual changes to the ping role. If the
role update stays pending, ask an administrator to check
`/config working-hours show`.

### I can’t set a schedule

If you rejoined the server or regained an eligible role, submit your hours
again. If Sylvan reports that scheduling is disabled or you need an eligible
role, contact an administrator. If it says member data is unavailable and
*no preference was changed*, try again once Sylvan has reconnected.
