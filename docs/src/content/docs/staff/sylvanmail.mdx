---
title: Reply to ModMail
description: Read incoming mail, prepare a reply and choose whether the member sees your username.
---

Open the member’s post in the private mail forum. The first message gives their username and user ID. If tickets are enabled, the post can also include their [recent ticket history](../tickets/find-a-ticket/#in-a-modmail-conversation).

Anyone who can write in the post can reply. The notification role gets a ping when a case opens and again when the member writes after a gap of at least three hours between their DMs.

## Prepare a reply

Mention **Sylvan** in the body of your message, or use **Reply** on a message from Sylvan and leave the reply ping on. Write what you want the member to receive and attach any files. An ordinary message to colleagues stays in the post.

Posting prepares a draft. You can still edit the text and attachments. Wait for Sylvan to add three reactions to the draft, then choose one yourself. Only the author can send or cancel it.

| Reaction | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 🕴️ **Send anonymously** | ModMail sends the reply with **From staff:**. |
| 🧍 **Send with your username** | ModMail sends the reply with **From** followed by your Discord username. |
| ❌ **Cancel** | The draft stays in the staff post. Edit it to prepare it again. |

Sylvan strips its own mention from the reply. Other mentions in the delivered copy do not ping anyone. A reply can contain up to 16,000 text characters and 10 ordinary attachments, with a limit of 8 MiB per file and 24 MiB altogether. Long text arrives in parts.

## Check delivery

A ✅ on the draft confirms that Discord accepted the whole reply. Later edits or deletions leave the delivered copy in place. To correct a sent reply, prepare a new one.

If a draft has only the old 📤 reaction, edit it to get the current controls. If no controls appear, check that you mentioned Sylvan or replied to one of its messages with the ping on, and that you are in the current mail forum.

For a delivery error, read the notice in the case post. If the reply was partially delivered or its outcome is uncertain, check with the member before sending any part again. Sylvan does not automatically resend an interrupted reply.
