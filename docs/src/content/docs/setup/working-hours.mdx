---
title: Configure the moderator ping role
description: Give moderators access to working hours, set the default for staff without a schedule, and pause or resume scheduling.
---

You need **Administrator** to configure Working Hours. Use a separate role
for pings so moderators keep their staff permissions outside their hours.

<span id="check-the-current-configuration" />
<span id="config-working-hours-show" />

Run `/config working-hours show` in the server. The private reply lists
the ping role, eligible staff roles, exemptions and any problems Sylvan
can identify. If scheduling is already enabled, [pause it](#pause-or-resume-scheduling)
before replacing the ping role.

<span id="set-up-the-roles" />
<span id="config-working-hours-ping-role" />

## Choose the roles

1. Choose the role that members will mention, such as `@Moderators`. Give
   Sylvan **Manage Roles** and put its highest role above the ping role.
   Select an ordinary server role. Sylvan rejects `@everyone`, roles
   managed by an integration and roles already marked eligible.

   ```text
   /config working-hours ping-role role:@Moderators
   ```

2. Add each staff role whose holders should receive pings. Select a role
   other than `@everyone` or the ping role. Staff also need **Mute Members**
   to set their own hours.

   ```text
   /config working-hours eligible-role action:add role:@Trial Moderators
   ```

<span id="set-exemptions-for-staff-without-a-schedule" />
<span id="config-working-hours-exempt-role" />

## Choose who manages the role without a schedule

By default, eligible staff with no schedule receive the ping role all day.
To let holders of a particular staff role manage their ping role manually
instead, make that staff role eligible first, then add its exemption.

```text
/config working-hours exempt-role action:add role:@Senior Moderators
```

An exempt moderator can still set [personal working hours](../../staff/working-hours/).
While their schedule is enabled and running, Sylvan manages their ping role.

<span id="config-working-hours-enable" />

## Enable and verify

Run `/config working-hours enable`, then `/config working-hours show`.
Check that scheduling is enabled, the correct ping role is listed and at
least one staff role is eligible. If a saved change is still pending,
check the bot’s **Manage Roles** permission and role position. If the reply
says member data is unavailable, check again after Sylvan reconnects.

<span id="config-working-hours-eligible-role" />

## Remove a staff role

Use `/config working-hours exempt-role action:remove role:@Senior Moderators`
to end an exemption. To remove a role from Working Hours altogether,
remove its exemption first, then run `/config working-hours eligible-role`
with `action:remove` and the same role. A moderator who loses their last
eligible role loses the ping role and their personal schedule is disabled.
Pause scheduling before removing the last eligible role on the server.

<span id="enable-or-suspend-scheduling" />
<span id="config-working-hours-disable" />

## Pause or resume scheduling

Run `/config working-hours disable` to pause the server’s schedules.
Eligible staff receive the ping role throughout the pause. Exempt staff
keep manual control. Sylvan retains the saved hours. Run
`/config working-hours enable` to resume them at the current time.

If you replace the ping role during a pause, review the old role’s
assignments separately. Members who held it still have it.
