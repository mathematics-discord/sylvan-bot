Last updated 10 November 2026

Sylvan is the moderation and community bot for the Mathematics Discord server. These terms cover Sylvan and its companion account, SylvanMail. Both
are operated by **ratgirl**. You can contact the operator privately at [rattus-ratgirl@protonmail.com](mailto:rattus-ratgirl@protonmail.com).

## Using Sylvan

When you use a Sylvan command, choose a role or message SylvanMail, you agree to these terms. You must meet Discord’s minimum age for your country and
follow the Mathematics server rules, [Discord’s Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and
its [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

Some features run whenever you participate in Mathematics, even if you never use a command. Sylvan can check messages against moderation rules, record
edits and deletions in configured channels, and keep a history of moderation actions. The [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/) explains what
it records and how to exercise your rights.

## Contacting staff

Send a DM to [SylvanMail](https://discord.com/users/1567888927048863784) to report a problem or ask staff to review a moderation action. Your message
and supported attachments go to a staff forum. A delivery acknowledgement means the message reached that forum. A moderator may read and answer it
later.

Provide the context staff need. Do not use the bot to harass someone, impersonate another person, submit deliberately false reports, or distribute
someone’s private information without a legitimate reason. An honest report is welcome even if staff reach a different conclusion. If you cannot use
SylvanMail, you can contact the operator by email.

## Staff use

Use moderation commands and records only for server duties you are authorised to perform. Check the destination before looking up a ticket. Both
`/ticket show` and `/ticket showhidden` post their results where the command was used. Keep ticket details, logs and ModMail conversations within the
audience that needs them to handle the case.

Do not bypass access controls, interfere with the bot’s operation, or use staff access to collect information about members for unrelated purposes.
Misuse may result in loss of access to bot features or moderation under the server rules.

## Availability and changes

Discord outages, permissions or bot downtime can delay a reply, a log entry or the lifting of a restriction. If a restriction appears to have remained
in place past its expiry, contact staff and include the ticket number if you have it.

The operator may change or retire features. Changes to these terms will appear on this page with a revised date. These terms do not limit rights you
have under applicable law.
